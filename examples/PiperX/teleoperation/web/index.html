Controls

Right grip (hold)Move the arm. Release to re-position your hand ("clutch").
Right triggerClose the gripper while held.
Right ANext step: move home → start recording → save episode.
Right BDiscard the episode being recorded.
Left YSlowly move the arm to the home pose.
Left XAlign the frame: point the right controller's red X axis toward robot +X (forward) and press.
Right stick clickShow / hide the alignment aids: the real gripper's orientation (translucent axes at the right controller) and the robot base axes (above the left controller).

Keyboard on the robot PC: Space/→ = A, Backspace/← = B, h = home, q/Esc = quit.
